Timeline

The project starts as one line at kickoff, then splits into propulsion, mechanical and electrical branches. Shared entries bridge the branches they involve; whole-team entries span all three. On a phone, pick one branch at a time.

  1. "Halo" Project Kickoff!

    Summer 2025 Inspired by Gruyere's VTVL, optimists took to the challenge

  2. First Design of N2O/IPA Engine

    1.25kN engine designed for short duration testing, requiring design of upstream system to support static testing

  3. Mojave Sphinx Launch

    In pursuit of more complex vehicles

    1. Parts ordered
    2. Integration begins
    3. Final integration, launch prep.

  4. Sights Set on Lander Challenge

    Vehicle redesigned for VTVL operation and 40 second burn with nominal thrust of 2.5kN and max thrust of 3kN at sea level

  5. Feed System to Support 3kN

    Designed for 3kN, N2O/IPA, 3:1 throttling

  6. Halo Jr. Inception

    Dual-rotor, single motor vehicle begins design

  7. Halo Design Review

    3 hour presentation given outlining reasoning for all design choices thus far; feedback from launch and hardware experts

  8. Halo v5

    CAD model including all propulsion systems and ground support systems, cold gas thrusters, TVC, and structures published

  9. Workshop Acquired!

    Santa Barbara Hackerspace (SBHX) graciously rents GRP a shed for storage and work

  10. Funding Delays...

    Construction halts due to lack of funding. high pressure infrastructure for cold flow tests inaccessible

  11. URCA

    Five posters presented at URCA fair and later selected to be featured at the UCSB library!

    1. Propulsion displays valve assemblies, and throttle and injector concepts
    2. Avionics displays simulated algorithms and Halo Junior design

  12. "Ortus", Halo Test Platform Inception

    Learn-by-doing: Ortus scale systems allow for rapid iteration at a significantly lower cost; experimental Halo designs tested

    1. Sensor Integration
    2. Ortus Injector (v1)
    3. Static Venturi Throttle Test
    4. Supercharge
    5. Hotfire Stand
    6. TVC
    7. Regenerative Cooled Engine
    8. Ortus Injector (v2)

  13. Fall FAR 1: Scrubbed

    Scrubbed hotfire due to poor test stand design and preparation

  14. Fall FAR 3: Cold Flow

    Hotfire turned cold flow due to poor launch ops

  15. Fall FAR 6: 2x Static Fires!

    First successful hot fire!!!

    1. Hotfire of stock Mojave Sphinx
    2. Hotfire of Ortus Injector (v1)
    3. First data acquisition

  16. Halo Jr. Maiden Flight!

    Halo Jr. flies for the first time; successfully maintains altitude!

    1. First flight and Hover
    2. Controlled ascent to 5 ft, hover, and descent
    3. Controlled flight with specifications given for hovering and landing locations.

  17. Fall FAR 8: Injector (v1), Engine Static

    Hotfire of Injector (v1), graphite throat, phenolic nozzles

  18. Fall Quarter Ends

  19. Fall FAR 10: Injector (v2), Engine Static

    Hotfire of Injector (v2), Engine (v1)

  20. Halo Avionics Distributed System

    Designs for all PCBs to be used and tested on halo jr. then scaled and edited for Halo.

    1. Motor board to be used in ignition/TVC
    2. RTK integration with a ground station and satellite
    3. Parts ordered to begin in house manufacturing of PCBs for Halo

  21. Winter FAR 0: Ortus TVC

    Ortus Supercharge and TVC static tests

  22. Winter FAR 2: Short Duration 3kN Static

  26. Winter FAR 7: Variable Duration 3kN Static

  27. Winter FAR 9: Full 40s 3kN Static
