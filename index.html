About us

Gaucho Rocket Project is UC Santa Barbara's Premire Student Ran Organization. We strive to push the limits of what studetns can accomplish.

We are driven to accomplish Two Goals: Be the First American Team to Land a Rocket & Launch More Rockets than any other Collegiate Team

No experience is needed to join. Bring curiosity and we will teach you the rest, whether you are into mechanical design, electronics, software, or running a team.
