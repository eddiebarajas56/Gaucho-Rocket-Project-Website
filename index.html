About us

We are a student team at UC Santa Barbara that designs, builds, and launches rockets. Everything we fly, from the airframe to the avionics, is made by students.

No experience is needed to join. Bring curiosity and we will teach you the rest, whether you are into mechanical design, electronics, software, or running a team.
